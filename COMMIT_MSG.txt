fix: set miniBarStyle EXPAND so tool capsule renders at full width

Root cause: HdsTabs miniBar defaults to COLLAPSE state (tiny circle at
bottom-right). The miniBarWidth breakpoint was set but ignored because
the bar was in collapse mode. Setting miniBarStyle: HdsBarStyle.EXPAND
forces the miniBar to render at its full miniBarWidth (200vp) as a
proper tool capsule alongside the reply capsule. Also added
barLayoutMode: HORIZONTAL (API 24+) to ensure both capsules are
arranged in one row.
